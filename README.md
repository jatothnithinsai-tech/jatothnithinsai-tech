## About Me

I'm a data analytics professional currently pursuing my **Management in Data Analytics**, with hands-on experience building end-to-end analytics projects across healthcare, retail, ecommerce, hospitality, and transport.

My work is focused on clean, reproducible pipelines — structured SQL, well-documented Python notebooks, and dashboards designed for real decision-makers. I care about turning messy data into findings that are clear, honest, and actionable.

Open to **Data Analyst**, **Business Analyst**, and **BI Analyst** roles.
